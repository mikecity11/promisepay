Name your plan.

Choose personal savings or a team plan. Add teammates with their wallet address and the USDC amount they’re owed.

Get everyone on board.

A few helpful answers.

Can I withdraw my money?

Yes. Choose Withdraw from your available balance or savings, enter an amount, and approve it in your wallet. The funds are sent to that connected account.

Does this work with every wallet transfer?

Currently, savings apply only to payments you add through a PromisePay plan. PromisePay does not monitor or deduct from unrelated wallet transfers.

Do I need to know an agreement number?

No. Your plans and invitations appear automatically after connecting. Select the plan by its name.

What does testnet mean?

This version uses test NEAR and test USDC. They are for testing and have no real-money value. You need some test NEAR for transaction fees; creating a plan also uses 0.1 test NEAR for storage.

Does my savings balance earn interest?

Not yet. The savings feature sets funds aside, but an interest-earning protocol is not connected.
